# Omid Saghatchian

I enjoy solving complex problems by examining their assumptions and breaking them into simpler parts. In my research, I study how large language models reason and how to evaluate their security. I also work on making diffusion models more efficient.

## Selected research

- **SCRBench** — We introduce a benchmark and use it to evaluate how language models reason about code changes. The paper is under review at NAACL. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41562)
- **CA-ToMe** — I implemented a method in Stable Diffusion that speeds up image generation by reusing token matches across denoising steps. [Code](https://github.com/omidiu/ca_tome) · [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00946)
